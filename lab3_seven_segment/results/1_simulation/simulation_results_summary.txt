Simulation-only parameter overrides (the lab asks to shorten the push-button time to ~100 ns in simulation):
  tb_chip_top     : chip_top #(.MAX_TICK(2), .MAX_COUNT(5))   -> tick every 2 clk, "1 s" = 5 ticks = 10 clk = 100 ns
  tb_btn_hold_1s  : btn_hold_1s #(.MAX_COUNT(2)), TB tick every 5 clk -> "1 s" = 2 ticks = 10 clk
  tb_tick_gen     : tick_gen #(.MAX_TICK(5))
  Real hardware   : MAX_TICK = 50000 (100 MHz / 50000 = 2 kHz scan tick), MAX_COUNT = 2000 (2000 ticks = 1.000 s)
